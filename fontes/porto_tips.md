Porto tips

Best Sunsets:🔆

Jardim do Morro
If you’re in the city center, walk across the upper deck of the Dom Luís I Bridge (the metro bridge) to Vila Nova de Gaia and watch the sunset from Jardm do Morro. Probably the best panoramic view of Porto. It’s very crowded, so it’s not my favorite. But the view is absolutely worth it. 
 https://maps.app.goo.gl/hr3NMajXmc1avT386 

Jardim das Virtudes 
A terraced park with several casual bars where you can sit with a beer and enjoy a beautiful sunset over the Douro. 
https://maps.app.goo.gl/kzzHN7K9URZgd5Hf8 

Parks: 🌳

Parque de Serralves
A paid-entry and truly stunning park (20 euros). The ticket also includes access to the Serralves Museum of Contemporary Art, which is definitely worth a visit. For me, it’s one of those must-see places that you shouldn’t miss when exploring Porto.
https://maps.app.goo.gl/XgHi4pFyLWz2mxY5A

Parque da cidade
It's another place I highly recommend. It’s a free, sprawling park located in the Matosinhos area (Porto’s coastal district). It’s worth taking the time to explore its green paths and lakes, and then continue all the way to where the park meets the beach. While you’re there, don’t miss the Anémona, an impressive public art installation in a nearby roundabout.  Since you’ll be in Matosinhos I suggest exploring the area on foot and enjouy a meal at one of its many restaurants, ideally specializing in seafood (the region’s specialty). It’s the perfect place to try bacalhau (cod ) or "polvo à lagareiro" (octopus) prepared in the traditional Portuguese way. 
https://maps.app.goo.gl/HwTq2b2iXiyhMSdJ8 

Jardins do Palácio de Cristal
It's a free and enchanting green oasis perched above the city of Porto, offering breathtaking panoramic views over the Douro River, the cityscape, and even the sea https://maps.app.goo.gl/EBkStk2u8WtU7qjr6

Restaurants and Snacks 🍴
Brasão Porto has a few traditional dishes worth mentioning, and the most famous is the Francesinha  (a hearty sandwich with layers of meat, covered in melted cheese and a rich sauce). I’m not a fan myself, so it’s hard for me to recommend, but I’ve heard many say the best one is at Brasão, which has a location in the city center.
https://maps.app.goo.gl/d8VY67NbtYtUiYyz7

Vamos Mais 5
Other local favorites include the "Prego no Pão com Queijo", basically a beef and cheese sandwich. But it's really good and special
https://maps.app.goo.gl/f7KTmCrjNx24QP9D9
Conga Casa das Bifanas The best bifanas, in my opinion. It's a traditional dish, a pork sandwich served with an incredible, slightly spicy sauce. https://maps.app.goo.gl/ePwjDfTGso3NxsDe7

Restaurante 🍽️ Marisqueira Serpa Pinto This restaurant serves incredibly fresh cod and octopus, and it’s one of my favorites in the Matosinhos area . The same neighborhood I mentioned earlier as worth exploring on foot. https://maps.app.goo.gl/cwEpmPm4ARqmP8FL7

Museu d'Avó 
I highly recommend this tapas restaurant for its full-on, immersive atmosphere. It’s a Spanish-style tapas bar offering a great variety of dishes—perfect for sharing and sampling a little bit of everything. Be warned: reservations are absolutely mandatory.

https://maps.app.goo.gl/gXHtRA5yUTWYtLja8

Manteigaria 
One thing you can’t leave Portugal without trying is pastéis de nata (the famous Portuguese custard tarts with a flaky pastry shell and creamy, slightly caramelized filling). In Porto, my top pick is Manteigaria, which serves them fresh out of the oven throughout the day. You can even watch the bakers preparing them right behind the counter. Crisp, warm, and perfectly balanced in sweetness. They’re, in my opinion, the best in the city. 
https://maps.app.goo.gl/RKtNYmEiAUC1kWtS6
PS: It’s located very close to two iconic Porto spots: the Mercado do Bolhão, a historic market recently renovated, and Rua de Santa Catarina, a pedestrian street that’s well worth walking along for its shops, cafés, and lively atmosphere.
This street is also home to the famous Café Majestic, known for its stunning architecture and long-standing tradition. In my opinion, though, it’s not worth the high prices they charge for anything there.

Bars:🍸

Bar Aduela
Is one of those charming spots set in the heart of downtown Porto. By day, it’s perfect for a relaxed coffee or a light lunch on its outdoor terrace. At night, the atmosphere comes alive, with good music, friendly chatter, and a lively flow of people. The drinks menu features a great selection of Portuguese wines, as well as craft beers, cocktails, and sangria. For food, you’ll find tasty toasts, conservas, and other small traditional snacks. 
https://maps.app.goo.gl/MxBaHrap4C1pN97M8

Terraplana Bar
It's a great spot for cocktails, with a laid-back, welcoming atmosphere and an amazing vinyl collection that sets the tone for the whole place. It’s the kind of bar where you can enjoy a well-crafted drink while surrounded by good music and a cozy vibe.
https://maps.app.goo.gl/r4BHDFncSvSgs24o6

Meridians & Parallels – Cocktail Bar
Serves excellent drinks and has a lovely outdoor area that makes it perfect for enjoying a cocktail in a relaxed, open-air setting. 
https://maps.app.goo.gl/zb5ADaQKdXEaxMV18

Beers:🍺
Armazém da Cerveja
For craft beer lovers, Porto has some great spots. Armazém da Cerveja offers a wide selection of beers on tap and has a pleasant outdoor seating area. https://maps.app.goo.gl/TJzJ5H5X8PhfAhQL6

Sigilo 
it may look like just a small doorway from the street, but head downstairs and you’ll find a beautiful hidden garden, perfect for enjoying a beer along with some snacks or a burger.
https://maps.app.goo.gl/SwL96YAKPwbi5vSy8

Espaço 77
If you want a more local (and maybe slightly younger and livelier) experience at a lower cost, I recommend Espaço 77. The bar that sells the most Super Bock (Portugal’s most popular beer) in the entire country. They also serve great snacks, from bolinhos de bacalhau (cod fritters) to tasty Brazilian-style bites.
https://maps.app.goo.gl/PuB7AqePFhrdjKcS7

Clubs:🕺

Galerias de Paris
If you enjoy bars and clubs, the best area to go out in Porto is Galerias de Paris. (https://maps.app.goo.gl/GLsDHaB2CSZqnwiy5) Just keep in mind that nightlife here starts late (rarely before 11 p.m), and can go on until morning if you’re up for it. You’ll find plenty of bars and clubs with different music styles to suit all tastes.
Two of my favorites are Plano B (https://maps.app.goo.gl/qiwupzcyVZCGu7qo9), which plays electronic music in one room and pop in another, and Tendinha dos Clérigos (https://maps.app.goo.gl/Y3fDzA4BB2SPAowcA), a rock bar that only really gets lively later in the night, around 1 a.m.


Other things: 
These recommendations don’t include churches, as I’ve never visited any myself and I don’t usually frequent those kinds of places. However, many people do recommend Porto’s Sé Cathedral, the Clérigos Tower, and the Igreja do Carmo as must-see landmarks for their architecture, history, and city views.

Enjoy!! 🇵🇹
